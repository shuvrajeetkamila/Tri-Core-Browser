Electron and the bundled Chromium runtime
-----------------------------------------
The Windows application is packaged with Electron 44.7.0 and its Chromium/V8 runtime. Electron and Chromium include third-party components and notices. Electron's upstream Electron license and Chromium license manifest are retained by electron-builder in the packaged runtime. The portable ZIP also contains the upstream files under `third-party/LICENSE.electron.txt` and `third-party/LICENSES.chromium.html`.

Build-only JavaScript tools
---------------------------
Vite 8.3.4 and electron-builder 26.17.0, along with their locked transitive packages, are used only to build/test/package the application. Their package licenses and dependency inventory are recorded in `package-lock.json` and the CI `dependency-tree.json` artifact.

Graphics SDKs and shader sources
--------------------------------
No NVIDIA Streamline/DLSS SDK/runtime/plugin, DLSS model or weight file, or graphics-driver DLL is bundled. The source tree includes only the minimum official AMD FidelityFX FSR 1.0.2 shader inputs used by the isolated D3D12 diagnostic helper: `ffx_a.h`, `ffx_fsr1.h`, and the sample wrapper `FSR_Pass.hlsl`, pinned to `GPUOpen-Effects/FidelityFX-FSR` commit `a21ffb8f6c13233ba336352bdff293894c706575`. Their AMD MIT license, original notices, third-party attributions, upstream paths, and SHA-256 hashes are retained in `native/fsr1-poc/third_party/amd/` and beside the helper in a packaged build. This source inclusion does not imply arbitrary-page or real-time FSR integration. DLSS remains unavailable; the FSR helper is a bounded capturePage PNG test path only.
