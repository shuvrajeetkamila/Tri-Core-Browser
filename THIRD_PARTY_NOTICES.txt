Electron and the bundled Chromium runtime
-----------------------------------------
The Windows application is packaged with Electron 44.7.0 and its Chromium/V8 runtime. Electron and Chromium include third-party components and notices. Electron's upstream Electron license and Chromium license manifest are retained by electron-builder in the packaged runtime. The portable ZIP also contains the upstream files under `third-party/LICENSE.electron.txt` and `third-party/LICENSES.chromium.html`.
